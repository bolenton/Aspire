# Lantern Orchard and Ember server — 2026-09-07

The current build includes a fourth chapter, Lantern Orchard: eight new activities, Luma the owl, a moon harp and new song, a moon seed, and a firefly nest restoration. The full journey now contains 21 ordered activities. Six new original GLB assets expand the walkable world westward. Luma acknowledges nearby players; Ember has listening/thinking poses and speech-driven jaw/ear animation. Existing saves continue into the new chapter.

A separate Dockerized Go companion service now runs on Epyst-intel, sharing Conduit's existing Whisper and Piper containers and using the installed `gemma4:12b` model. Private HTTPS port 8443 leaves Conduit's existing route intact. The client supplies authoritative world/quest/journal context, uses streamed sentence audio and automatic conversational turn-taking, and retains its on-device fallback. See [conversation architecture](UnityConversation.md).

Verification so far: 20 C# checks, Go race/integration checks, all four GitHub CI jobs, all 21 activities through actual Editor and build 12 iPhone navigation/tap/chime handlers, successful Unity iOS export and signed native Xcode build. Live synthetic Whisper → contextual Ollama → Piper and follow-up memory passed against the deployed server. All seven changed/new character/Orchard GLBs match the packaged app bytes. Physical-device results are tracked in [UnityDeviceCheck](UnityDeviceCheck.md).

Orange now runs the persistent voice-mode revision: tap once to enable, talk across repeated turns, tap again to disable. A large animated orb distinguishes capture, preparation and speech. Movement preserves the mode, smaller drags respond sooner, and native audio startup/decoding no longer run on the Unity frame thread. Physical native capture, automatic turn continuation and explicit-off shutdown passed on final build 12. Final build and frame evidence is maintained in [UnityDeviceCheck](UnityDeviceCheck.md). The iPad is currently unavailable. Recognition accuracy in a real spoken family conversation remains a separate acceptance check. This is an expanded playable build, not a declaration of completed AAA production quality.

## Current presentation and controls

- Original Blender-authored explorer, Ember, lantern oak, terrain, plants, stream, bridge and acorn, exported as seven GLB assets. Named limb pivots provide walking poses, breathing, blinking and tail motion. Ember follows after the introduction.
- New river bell, sunflower gateway, lantern flower, garden star, planted garden and seating. The original oak quest continues into the river/garden chapter and a return-home chapter: 13 ordered activities total. The player can keep exploring and replay songs afterward. Existing build 4 progress continues with the same quest/save identifiers.
- URP daylight, a sky, atmospheric distance, shadows and color grading. Soft sky fill, restrained bloom, slow leaf motion, moving water and subtle surface variation enrich the scene. A calm camera keeps both characters prominent and clears the arch/trunk bounds. Collectibles float at a readable height; Ember chooses the other side of the current destination. No camera shake, motion blur or depth of field.
- Touch anywhere to establish a floating joystick. Drag direction controls walking; lifting stops. A second finger cannot relocate or release the active control. World taps use the visible object bounds, with forgiving near misses. The gateway opening passes taps through to objects beyond it. A world-space ring marks the current destination without text.
- Three small persistent icons: pause, guided travel and voice. Captions are off by default; full words remain available through speech, VoiceOver and the pause menu. Persistent joystick visibility and captions are separate saved options.
- Broad walkable space and obstacle-aware assisted travel, including a smooth safe crossing over the arched bridge. Default walking is 2.8 m/s (70% faster than build 4), and guidance is 3.22 m/s (95% faster). Saved Gentle/Comfortable/Brisk options are available. Step and turn operations are gradual; pauses and focus loss stop movement.
- Songs use large marked touch chimes over the world, with spoken labels, progress dots, replay, and no time limit. Marks distinguish notes without relying on color.
- New Scott Buckley piano/strings score, Thimras park field recordings, Kenney grass footsteps and bell recordings. Narration ducks other sound; only the current destination plays a guidance beacon. No legacy audio, music or sample character model is packaged. See [asset sources](UnityAssetLedger.md).
- Illustrated title artwork is distinct from real gameplay rendering. The Blender preview renders are asset previews, not proof of Unity or device appearance.
