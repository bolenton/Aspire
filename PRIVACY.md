# Lantern Privacy Policy

**Lantern collects no data. None.**

Lantern is a story adventure designed for visually impaired children. It was
built for one child and is shared free in her honor, so its privacy posture
is the simplest possible one:

## What stays on the device (everything)

- **Game progress, choices, and memories** — saved only in a local file on
  the device. Never transmitted, never synced by us.
- **The child's first name** — entered by a parent, used only so the
  companion can say it aloud. Stored locally, never transmitted.
- **Voice ("talk to your companion")** — speech recognition runs on-device
  using Apple's frameworks wherever the device supports it. Listening only
  happens after an explicit tap, with audible start/stop sounds. Audio is
  not recorded, stored, or sent to us — we have no servers.
- **Headphone motion** (optional, for sound that stays anchored in the
  world) — processed in the moment, never stored or transmitted.

## What the app never has

- No accounts. No analytics. No advertising. No tracking of any kind.
- No third-party SDKs.
- No in-app purchases.
- No data leaves the device to us — we operate no servers.

## The one optional exception, controlled by parents

Behind the parent gate, a parent may connect the companion to an AI server
**they choose and control** (for example, a computer in their own home, or a
provider they have an account with). When — and only when — a parent
configures this:

- the child's spoken questions and the game's current situation are sent to
  **that parent-chosen server** to generate the companion's answers;
- that data goes only to the configured server, governed by that server's
  privacy practices, which the parent controls or has accepted;
- removing the server address in settings stops this entirely. The game is
  fully playable without it.

By default this is **off**, and the built-in offline storyteller answers.

## Children's privacy

Lantern is designed for children and complies with children's privacy law
(including COPPA) the straightforward way: by collecting nothing. Settings
and the optional AI-server configuration sit behind a parental gate.
