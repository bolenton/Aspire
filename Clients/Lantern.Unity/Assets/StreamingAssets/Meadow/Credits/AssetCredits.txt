LANTERN MEADOW — ART AND AUDIO

Explorer, Ember, the meadow, lantern oak, river bell, lantern garden and wishing star:
Original meshes authored for Lantern in Blender. Reproducible source scripts:
scripts/art/geometry.py, characters.py, meadow.py, garden.py and build_assets.py.
Character movement uses named pose pivots, not motion-capture animation.

Title illustration: generated for Lantern using OpenAI image generation.
This illustration appears only on the title screen; it is not a gameplay capture.

Impact Sounds: Kenney, CC0. The original license accompanies this file.
https://kenney.nl/assets/impact-sounds
Grass footsteps and bell recordings are filtered, leveled and resampled for Lantern.
Musical notes are pitched from the newly acquired recorded bell sample.

No audio, character models or environment models from the old Lantern client
are included in this meadow's presentation.
